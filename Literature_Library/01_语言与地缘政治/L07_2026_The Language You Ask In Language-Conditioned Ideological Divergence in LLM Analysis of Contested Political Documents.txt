B.5   Research Methodology
Researchers using LLMs for multilingual analy-
sis should recognize that prompt language may
condition results. Studies comparing political phe-
nomena across linguistic contexts should control
for this possibility, potentially by running analy-
ses in multiple prompt languages and examining
convergence. Our findings also highlight the im-
portance of documenting prompt language in LLM
research; reproducibility requires specification not
just of model version and prompt text but of the
language in which prompts were administered.
   More broadly, scholars using LLMs as analytical
tools should treat their vocabulary and evaluative
