Figure 1: Methodological scheme. (1) Bots collected data from search engines (Google, Bing, Duck-
DuckGo, Yahoo) and LLMs (Copilot, ChatGPT), from different locations within the European Union
(Austria - AU, Germany - DE, Ireland - IR, Poland - PL, and Portugal - PT) or from different counties
in the United States of America. For LLMs, location was simulated by prompting the model in the most
spoken language of each country (EU only), rather than through actual geolocation. (2) Political entities
were extracted from the results collected in (1), i.e. from URLs and headlines for SEs and from textual
answers for LLMs. (3) These entities were mapped to their ideological leanings using their placement on
political spectra in the case of the EU, or according to the issues considered more important to Democratic
or Republican voters in the case of the US. (4) The observed leanings were compared with external factors
(media salience, polls, prior electoral results).
